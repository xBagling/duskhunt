A daily animal sound quiz

Every day there's one mystery animal, the same for everyone. You hear its real call, slowed down to 1/8 speed, where even a mouse can sound like a whale. Name it before the sun goes down.

How to play

  1. Listen. Press Start and hear today's call at 1/8 speed: deep, slow and strange.
  2. Guess. Type any animal. Breeds and nicknames work, and so do a few silly things.
  3. Get closer. Every miss speeds the call up (1/4×, 1/2×, then real speed) and shows how close you were: same class, same order, same family.
  4. Beat the dark. At dusk its shape appears on the ridge. After six tries, it gets away.

Questions

Is Dusk Hunt free?

Yes. There's no sign-up and no account, and it plays in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

When is there a new animal?

Every midnight, your local time. Missed a day? Every earlier animal is waiting in Past days.

Why is the sound slowed down?

Slowing a sound drops its pitch, so at 1/8 speed a songbird can rumble like a lion. Most animals give themselves away somewhere between 1/4 speed and real speed.

Is there an easy or a hard mode?

Both. Easy mode is for the birds, insects and frogs nobody could name by ear: the everyday name is enough, like “toad” for an American toad, “owl” for a barn owl or “cricket” for a mole cricket. A cow is still a cow, though, and a lion isn’t a cat. Normal mode gives a little help with lookalikes, like an Arctic fox for a red fox. In hard mode only the exact species counts and every guess costs a try. The sounds are the same in every mode.

Can I play with animals from one part of the world?

Yes. Tap “Animals from the whole world” on the start screen and pick Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas or Oceania on the map. Each part of the world has its own animal every day, the same for everyone who picks it, and your browser remembers your choice. Pets and farm animals live everywhere, so they can turn up in any part.

Where do the animal sounds come from?

They're real recordings shared on Wikimedia Commons under free licences, and each one is credited after you play.
